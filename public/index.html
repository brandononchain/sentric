How It Works

Your agent gets smarter
in three steps

01

We watch

Watch Solana wallets through Helius. Successful swaps become timestamped records with transaction signatures. Availability and latency depend on your provider.

02

We score

Inspect a 0–100 heuristic score with its component breakdown. Unknown historical metrics stay neutral. Independent wallets trading in the same direction provide additional context.

03

Your agent acts

Connect through REST or MCP. Filter signals, compare consensus, and inspect freshness before deciding what deserves further research.

Under the Hood

Understand every signal

A transparent heuristic, not a probability of profit. Missing historical data contributes a neutral 50 to its component. Review the methodology →

40%

Position Size

Estimated trade value in USD, using token decimals and available prices. This is absolute size, not a share of the wallet’s portfolio.

20%

Hold History

Reserved for measured holding periods. Historical watchlist guesses are excluded; this component is neutral until reliable data is supplied.

15%

Historical PnL

Unknown by default. A partial swap history cannot establish realized returns, so Sentric does not publish a fabricated win rate.

15%

Rug Avoidance

Unknown by default. A missing price is not evidence of a rug pull. Missing history stays neutral in the score.

10%

Wallet Consensus

Distinct other wallets trading the same mint in the same direction within the recent window. Repeated trades from one wallet count once.
